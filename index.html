    Open Celtic Knowledge System

    Preserve the past.

    OpenOrdú is an open-source, collaborative platform for Celtic Polytheism — the Public Celtic Encyclopedia, rituals, and lore, built by contributors and licensed for reuse.

    Every contribution is peer-reviewed, version-controlled, and credited. Never have your work stolen.
