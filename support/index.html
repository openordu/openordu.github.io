    Vision & Mission

    The Openordú Project is a public resource guide supporting the practice of Celtic Paganism. Inspired by the Open Siddur project, it aims to create a free software toolkit and a text-base to generate a comprehensive website. It encourages sharing, learning, and the development of Celtic culture. Its components include source texts, praxes, and a user-friendly website interface. Open to contributors, the project also plans to build a Public Celtic Encyclopedia with a focus on practical application over theory.

    How Your Support Helps

    Your support is instrumental in helping us keep OpenOrdú as a freely accessible platform for all. Contributions are utilized in maintaining and upgrading our digital infrastructure, curating high-quality content, and ensuring the platform's resilience. Donations also aid us in translating the content into numerous languages and archiving it in countless data centers to ensure future generations have access to this rich heritage.

    Transparency & Accountability

    At OpenOrdú, we uphold the highest standards of transparency and accountability. Rest assured, all donations and resources are utilized responsibly in line with our mission. Detailed reports of our operations and expenditure are made available regularly to keep our supporters informed.

    Donation Options

    To support us financially, we offer multiple channels for your convenience. Donations can be made via credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer. We also welcome both one-time donations and recurring contributions, providing you with the flexibility to contribute as per your convenience. Please note that as OpenOrdú is a non-profit organization, your donations are tax-deductible.

    Non-Monetary Support

    We value all forms of support that contribute to our mission. You can help by volunteering your time, offering in-kind donations, or simply by sharing information about OpenOrdú on social media to help us reach a wider audience.

    Recognition of Supporters

    We deeply appreciate the generosity of our supporters and believe in acknowledging their contribution. Donors are listed on our platform, with special mentions for 'Gold Donors', 'Silver Donors', and so forth, based on the level of contribution.
