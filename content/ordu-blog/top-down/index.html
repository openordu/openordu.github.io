Bottom Up or Top Down Approach. Which is Better?

This is a layout to explore which method is best to determine the proper order of the creation of the guides section of this project.

Bottom Up Jump to heading ¶

  • Lay programs first, bard second, seer third, druid fifth

  • PCE throughout

Cons Jump to heading ¶

  • Take forever to get prieists to help you build the project.

Pros Jump to heading ¶

  • Get attention of laity by providing regular resources.

Top Down Jump to heading ¶

  • Druid first, seer second, bard thrid, lay programs last.

  • PCE throughout

Cons Jump to heading ¶

  • Take forever to get the attention of lay ppl, regular practitioners

Pros Jump to heading ¶

  • Attract priests to help you build the project faster as you go.

Stradle Approach Jump to heading ¶

  • Lay programs first, Druid programs 2nd, other programs afterward.

Cons Jump to heading ¶

  • Potential to have an unfinished product of laity and priests, but no witchy, mantic or bardic programs if the project runs out of steam before attracting contributors.

Pros Jump to heading ¶

  • Attract laity at the right time.

  • Build a priesthood which can help you build the remaining parts sooner.

Conclusion

I think the last option, a staggered approach has the least amount of risk, the most amount of benefit.
