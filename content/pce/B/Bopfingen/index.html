    Bopfingen

    [, , ', C, h, r, i, s, t, o, p, h, e, r, , G, o, d, w, i, n, ', , ] 2026-08-28 11:28:09 about 2 minutes

    Concept Place Bopfingen Viereckschanze Eger valley Baden-Württemberg Heidmühl

    Bopfingen is a prehistoric site in south-west Germany set in the later phase of the Iron Age before Rome. Celtic-speaking people lived in this part of the land. The site holds a settlement and a fenced square. This four-sided earthwork is the type called a Viereckschanze. Some think such places served ritual ends. Bopfingen stands in the Eger valley of Baden-Württemberg. The ground is a flattened fan of limestone gravel. The Heidmühl river carried down the slopes of the Alb hills. The lower valley floods, so the heap stayed dry and safe. The pre-Roman village stretched far across the fan. The square enclosure dates to about the second century bc. Its wall ran some eighty-three metres north and south. The eastern span was shorter. The inner court covered roughly five thousand square metres. Within it stood several large timber halls. The later enclosure seems to have been a wide farmstead. Such a place could also hold political and religious roles. It likely served as the seat of a local group. The finds therefore add to what we know of rural La Tène life.
