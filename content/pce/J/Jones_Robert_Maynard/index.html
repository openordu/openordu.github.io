    Jones, Robert Maynard

    Jones, Robert Maynard, who wrote as Bobi Jones, was born in 1929. He grew up in Cardiff, the capital city of Wales. His household spoke no Welsh, yet he became a scholar of that tongue. He ranked among the first people from such a home to reach a university chair of the subject. He taught at Carmarthen and later at the University of Wales. He wrote a great many poems and critical works in Welsh. He was a firm Calvinist and a tireless defender of the language. He studied the medieval Welsh romances grouped as the Tair Rhamant. He helped establish the Welsh academy and a group that pairs adults learning Welsh with fluent speakers. His long research traced the theory of the letter and criticism.
