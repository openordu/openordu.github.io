    Jenner, Henry

    [, , ', C, h, r, i, s, t, o, p, h, e, r, , G, o, d, w, i, n, ', , ] 2026-08-28 11:28:09 about 2 minutes

    Person Work Place Group Jenner, Henry Cornish language revival St Columb Major Charter Endorsement British Museum Library Newlyn Handbook of the Cornish Language Cornish Gorseth Robert Morton Nance

    Jenner, Henry (1848–1934) is remembered above all as a driving force for the Cornish revival. He was born in Saint Columb Major in mid-Cornwall, a place its people call Kernow. In the 1870s he took note of the oral Cornish traditions that still lived on in Penwith. While he worked in the British Museum Library he found the Charter Endorsement. It is a secular Middle Cornish writing of forty-one lines. In 1877 the clergyman Lach-Szyrma drew him into recording the Cornish that Newlyn fishermen still spoke. In 1904 he issued his Handbook of the Cornish Language at the urging of L. C. Duncombe-Jewell. That same year Cornwall gained full membership of the Celtic Association. The Federation of Old Cornish Societies rose in 1920 with his help and that of Robert Morton Nance. The Cornish Gorseth gave him its first Grand Bard honour in 1928. He had joined the Breton assembly nearly three decades earlier and wrote much verse in the revived tongue.
