    Diarmait Ua Duibne

    Christopher Godwin 2026-08-27 05:26:47 about 4 minutes

    place heroine creature race god goddess hero concept Diarmait UA DUIBNE DUBINN GRÁINNE FIONN MAC CUMHAILL FIANNA GEIS Ben Bulben Aongus Óg boar

    Diarmait UA DUIBNE descended from the obscure goddess DUBINN. He was the most famous mythological hero of this name. Diarmait was the fated lover of GRÁINNE. Gráinne was young and beautiful. She was betrothed to the aging FIONN MAC CUMHAILL. A magical woman gave Diarmait a love spot. The source names this mark the ball seirc. The spot sat on his forehead. It made him irresistible to women. The Fianna were a famed band of warriors. They hunted and fought across Ireland. Some tales hid the spot under bangs. A breeze lifted his hair at a feast in Fionn's hall. Gráinne spied the spot and fell in love. A GEIS forbade Diarmait from refusing a woman who came neither mounted nor on foot. She came at sundown riding a mule. This fulfilled the geis. Diarmait and Gráinne fled through Ireland. Fionn and the FIANNA pursued them. The stones where they slept were prehistoric DOLMENS. People call them beds of Diarmait and Gráinne. Legend counts 366 dolmens in Ireland. That number matches a leap year spent fleeing. Fionn caught the pair in the forest of Dubros in Co. Sligo. Diarmait hid in a HAZEL tree. A wild BOAR killed Diarmait on Ben Bulben. Only Fionn could heal that bleeding. The love-god Aonghus Óg of the Tuatha Dé fostered Diarmait. Aonghus Óg raised Diarmait as his own son. Diarmait had a half-brother. That boy was transformed into a boar. The boar was fated to kill Diarmait. Gráinne was the daughter of Cormac mac Airt. Cormac mac Airt was the legendary king of Tara. Gráinne drugged the celebrants at her wedding. The wedding would have joined her to Fionn. She put Diarmait under a sworn geis. The geis forced Diarmait to run off with her. Diarmait could not refuse the promise. Fionn and the Fianna chased the pair for sixteen years. The pursuit ran across all of Ireland. The long pursuit at last made peace. The peace did not hold for long. Years later Diarmait hunted with the Fianna. The wild boar gored Diarmait. Fionn could have saved his life. Fionn left Diarmait to die. Diarmait fell dead on Ben Bulben. His death fulfilled the old fate.

    • Diarmait UA DUIBNE: descended from the goddess DUBINN

    • GRÁINNE: the fated lover of Diarmait who saw his love spot

    • FIONN MAC CUMHAILL: the aging suitor who pursued the pair

    • Ben Bulben: place where a wild BOAR killed Diarmait
