    Conan Meriadoc

    [, , ', C, h, r, i, s, t, o, p, h, e, r, , G, o, d, w, i, n, ', , ] 2026-08-28 16:04:13 about 3 minutes

    legend figure place work Conan Meriadoc Geoffrey of Monmouth Historia Regum Britanniae Magnus Maximus Armorica Gildas House of Rohan

    Conan Meriadoc appears in Breton legend as a founder-hero. The oldest Breton stories credit him with leading the first colonial move into Armorica. Geoffrey of Monmouth gave the hero a leading share in his Historia Regum Britanniae, set down near 1139. Historians today doubt the old claim that Conan truly commanded the first Breton settlers in the 300s. The earliest clue sits in the Life of the Breton bishop Goueznou. Only its prologue survives, copied in the hand of the writer Pierre Le Baud. Some records say the text was drafted in 1019 and built on an older British story. That account tells how Britons under Brutus and their ally overcame the whole island. Then Conan Meriadoc crossed the sea to the citadel of Gaul with a great host of Britons. The rebellion of Magnus Maximus supplies the setting for the rest. Maximus came from Spain and rose up in the year 383 against Gratian, emperor of the West. He led an army out of the British provinces and gained power over Britain, Spain, Gaul, and much of North Africa. He fixed his court at Trier. The writer Gildas accused Maximus of draining Britain of its young soldiers. In this telling Conan carries the rank of leader over the British troops. Magnus then crowns Conan king of Armorica after the fall of the city of Rennes. The record counts thirty thousand soldiers and one hundred thousand civilians who moved to Armorica to found a new Britain across the sea. A related label, the Macsen Wledig, tells the same fable in Welsh dress. A Welsh line ties a figure called Kenan to the holy Cadac. Later Breton kings and noble houses such as the House of Rohan claimed descent from Conan. The cult of the saint Meriadec then kept his fame alive among the later dukes.

    • Conan Meriadoc: Legendary founder-hero hailed by most as first king of Armorica.

    • Geoffrey of Monmouth: Chronicler who placed Conan at the centre of his Historia Regum Britanniae.

    • Magnus Maximus: Rebel emperor whose reign in Britain anchors the whole Conan frame.

    • House of Rohan: Noble line that claimed descent from Conan and backed the Meriadec cult.
