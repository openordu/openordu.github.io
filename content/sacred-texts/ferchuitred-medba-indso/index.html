O doclos doergetar teglach rig Erenn immon ríg a Temraig amach & Tindi mac Connrach, & aderuit araili cur tuit Eochaid Dala le Tindi reime sin fon rigi, & ní fír sin. & tocbaidter meirgi rig Eredn d'innsaigi rig Ulad corfocrad comracc o Thinni mac Connruch ar Conchobur Monodhur mac Connrach derbbráithir Tindi, condebuirtt Conchobor fris Tindi do coscc.
