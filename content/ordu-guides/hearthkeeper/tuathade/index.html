Nemed(priviliged, holy) ⟴ *Iarbonel ⟴ Bethach(live-being, animal, beast) ⟴ *Ibath ⟴ *Baath ⟴ Edna-Énna(bird-like)[1] ⟴ Tavarn(see tabeo)[2] ⟴ Tat(to melt)[2:1]⟴ Aldui(Strong-friend)[3] ⟴ Ordam(bit, piece or dignity) ⟴ Etarlam(between) ⟴ Echtach(death-dealing/slayer) ⟴ Nuadu Airgetlam(cloud-maker Silver-arm) [3:1]

* These names don't seem Irish and are probably inserted. Iarbonel means descended from Jacob. ⟴ denotes 'father of'
