Glen Lyon — a living Beltane

Praxis Jump to heading ¶

In the Scottish Highlands, in the small valley of Glen Lyon in the Grampion mountains near Fortingall, an astonishing survival of the old Celtic ritual has continued into recent times. The valley keeps an age-old custom centred on the opening of summer at the Beltane tide, the first of May.

The rite is a living bearer of the fire: the seasonal hinge marked with fires on the approach of May Day, keeping the old festival's character in place long after the wider observance had faded. It stands as one of the clearest continuations of the Beltane fire-season in its home country.
