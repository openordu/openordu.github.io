Tlachtga

Praxis Jump to heading ¶

Tlachtga, now the Hill of Ward, is a mythological site and goddess of Ireland, less known than nearby Tara but of deep ritual meaning. It served as the site of the great oenach (assembly) of Samhain, the feast of winter's incoming at the opening of November.

The Hill of Ward is the ritual anchor of the season's hinge: the place where the year's turning was gathered, where the Celtic year's dark half was opened with solemn assembly. Its nearness to Tara, the high seat of the kings, tied the winter inauguration to the heart of the land.

Culture notes Jump to heading ¶

  • For the devout polytheist, Tlachtga is where the Samhain assembly belongs: the oenach of winter's beginning at the Hill of Ward.
