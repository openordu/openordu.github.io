Crom Dubh

Praxis Jump to heading ¶

Crom Dubh (Crobh Dearg) is an Irish deity and harvest figure whose close bounds run to the sacred spring attached to the farmstead, and to the local saint of the region, such as Gobnait. He is tied in the folk year to the great harvest-gathering season of Lughnasa, where he stands as the local keeper of the harvest blessing.

The well-cultus is his proper domain: his association with a sacred well on or near the farm, and with a local female saint, marks the ground as a place of veneration, healing, and the turning of the harvest.

Culture notes Jump to heading ¶

  • Practise the harvest-veneration at his festivals, tying the well to the local saint's tide. Observe with the historical Celtic seasonal calendar.
