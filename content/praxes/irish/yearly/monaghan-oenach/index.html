Oenach (Assembly)

Praxis Jump to heading ¶

The oenach was the Celtic annual assembly or fair, gathered at a named site on a named day of the year. It was the social and ritual hub of the seasonal calendar: the place where the tribe came together for games, trade, law, and the great festivals. The oenach was bound to the festival days — the assemblies of Samhain at Tlachtga, and the gatherings of the fire festivals.

The Line of these assemblies ran with the fire-year: the assembly was held at the seasonal hinge so that the whole people could mark the turning together.

Culture notes Jump to heading ¶

  • Practise the oenach as the yearly gathering of the community at the seasonal festival days — games, trade, and law conducted at the fire-feasts.

  • In the praxis matrix the oenach sits in the yearly and social quadrants, Irish and pan-Celtic rows.
