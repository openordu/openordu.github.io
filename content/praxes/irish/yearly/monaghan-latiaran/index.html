Latiaran

Praxis Jump to heading ¶

In the small town of Cullen in the north of County Cork stands a strange monument dedicated to Latiaran: an upright stone roughly heart-shaped, set near a diminutive holy well now dedicated to Saint Latiaran, a holy woman otherwise unknown.

The folk origin story runs that Latiaran was so modest that while she carried boulders in her apron, a smith praised the shape of her ankles, and she let the apron fall. The standing stone dropped out of it and wedged itself upright in the ground, and she vanished beneath it.
