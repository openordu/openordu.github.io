Garland Sunday

Praxis Jump to heading ¶

Garland Sunday is a summer Sunday of community gathering kept in Irish countryside, a day of the green open air when the roads, lanes, and stream-side brought people out of the houses and into the common ground. It belongs to the string of fairs and gatherings that thread the warm half of the year between the great festivals.

Its observance is one of assembly and of the shared table: garlands and foliage carried in procession, the community gathered on the common day, and the hospitality of the open air. It is a feast of the land's fruit and of the people meeting in it.

Culture notes Jump to heading ¶

  • A summer Sunday of gathering and relay, kept in the countryside.

  • Keep: the common ground, the shared table of the harvest season.
