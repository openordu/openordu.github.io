OpenOrdú Praxis

The praxis library of the OpenOrdú tradition — the historical Celtic festival calendar and the customs that hang on it, written for polytheists.

Organized by celtic culture, then by event cadence:

  • Culture roots: pan-celtic, irish, scottish, welsh, manx, cornish, breton, gaulish.

  • Event cadence under each culture: daily, weekly, monthly, seasonal, social, yearly, holidays, anthropological.

Each praxis entry carries: id, culture_tag, cadence, source citation, and an original prose body in own words. No modern syncretic-ritual framing — the calendar is the historical Celtic seasonal festival round (Samhain, Imbolc, Bealtaine, Lúnasa and the quarter-days/solstices).
