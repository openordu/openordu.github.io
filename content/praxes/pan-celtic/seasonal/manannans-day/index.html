Manannán's Day

Praxis Jump to heading ¶

Manannán's Day honours Mac Lir, the lord of the otherworld sea who stands at the border between the mortal world and the isle beyond — the same figure known in Mann as Manannan Beg mac y Leir, the only son of the wave. The feast sits in mid-summer, near the height of the warmth, a day of the sea and of the threshold.

For the devout polytheist the observance is one of the boundaries: of the shore, the tide, and the crossing. It is kept by honouring the sea that both divides and joins, by recalling the gifts of the wave-lord, and by marking the threshold in one's own life — the places where the world opens to what lies beyond.

Culture notes Jump to heading ¶

  • Honour of the sea-lord, kept near the height of summer.

  • Keep: the shore, the tide, and the thresholds that open.
