Midsummer (Meán Samhraidh)

Praxis Jump to heading ¶

Midsummer marks the height of the light, the longest day of the solar year when the sun stands at its zenith before the long turn back toward dark. The language conserves the middle-of-summer sense across the traditions — Irish Meán Samhraidh, Scottish Meadhan an t-samhraidh, Gaulish Medisamos, Cornish Golowan, Welsh Canol Haf.

For the devout polytheist it is a high feast of the light: a beacon kept, a witness to the standing sun, and the turning of the year acknowledged as the dark begins to lengthen again. The kindling of fires at the height of summer answers the fires of the depth of winter, the year held in balance at its two extremities.

Culture notes Jump to heading ¶

  • The summer solstice, the height of light between the fire festivals.
